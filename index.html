Our Story

Small business,
big heart.

Bayou Blossoms started with a passion for flowers and a garage — and that's exactly how we like it. We're a local Louisiana flower LLC serving the communities of Thibodaux and Luling with fresh, handpicked arrangements.

No big box store. No cold warehouse flowers. Just real people, real blooms, and genuine care poured into every bouquet we put together.
